Our Story

Why We Built CPP

We started Catalyst People Partners because we saw a gap in the market — too many executive search firms that move fast but don't think deeply, or think deeply but move too slowly.

Our founding team has sat on both sides of the table: as HR leaders in industry, as consultants advising boards, and as academics studying what makes organisations truly work. That combination is rare — and it's what makes our advice different.

We built CPP to be the firm we always wished existed: one that combines the speed and precision of a boutique with the intellectual rigour of a top-tier consultancy.

How We Work

Our Principles

Six things that guide every engagement we take on.

📋

Always Facts First

Every engagement starts with a rigorous, fact-based assessment of the situation. We don't skip this step — even when clients think they already know the answer.

🏗️

Implementation Mindset

We design solutions that can actually be executed in the real world — not perfect-on-paper recommendations that gather dust after the presentation.

🤝

Client-Based Recommendations

Initial recommendations are always prepared jointly with client teams. You know your business — we bring the expertise to shape it.

🔍

Critical Thinking

We challenge assumptions — including our own. Quality control is built into every deliverable, not added at the end.

🧠

Organisational Understanding

We bring deep knowledge of how organisations actually work — the formal structures and the informal dynamics that determine outcomes.

🚀

Practical Problem Solving

When something unexpected comes up — and it always does — we find a way through. Adaptability is a core part of how we operate.
